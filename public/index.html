MailConnect

Connect your team's email accounts

MailConnect is an email integration demonstration that helps teams connect their Gmail accounts to a centralized email workspace. Users can authorize their own accounts, view inbox messages, and send emails from the selected account.

Connected accounts can be organized by role, such as Owner, HR, and Accounts, to help teams manage their email workflows.

Gmail access is granted through Google's authorization page. MailConnect does not ask users to enter their Google password into this application.

Read our Privacy Policy to understand what information the application accesses and how it is handled.

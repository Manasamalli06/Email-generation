Privacy Policy

Email Chat Demo is a demonstration app that lets you connect your Gmail account to read your inbox and send email from one page.

Information we access

  • Your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account.
  • Your Gmail messages (to display your inbox and open emails) and permission to send email on your behalf.
  • The name, company and email you type into the form.

How we use it

This information is used only to show your inbox, display individual emails, and send emails you compose. It is not used for advertising and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Storage

Your sign-in tokens and details are kept only in the server's temporary session memory. They are not saved to a database and are removed when you click Disconnect, your session ends or the server restarts. Chat messages in the sample chat are also held in memory only.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove this app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

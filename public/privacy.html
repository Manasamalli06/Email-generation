Privacy Policy

Application: MailConnect

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. About this application

MailConnect is a demonstration application that lets users connect Gmail accounts to an email workspace, view inbox messages, and send emails from a connected account.

2. Information accessed

With your permission, the application accesses your Google profile information, including your name, email address, and profile picture. It also accesses Gmail messages to display inbox content and sends emails when you request that action.

3. How information is used

Information is used to identify the connected account, associate it with a selected role, display email messages, and send emails requested by the user. The application does not need your Google password.

4. Tokens and storage

OAuth tokens and connected-account information are handled by the application's server-side session. The current implementation uses in-memory session storage, so session information may be lost when the server restarts. Do not treat this demonstration as a production email service until its storage and security controls have been reviewed.

5. Sharing and service providers

The application uses Google OAuth and Gmail APIs to provide the requested features. It does not sell users' personal information. Google processes information as described in its own privacy policies and service terms.

6. Disconnecting and revoking access

You can disconnect an account from the application. Disconnecting removes the account information from the current application session. To revoke the application's Google access completely, visit your Google Account permissions .

7. Security

The application uses Google's authorization flow instead of asking users to provide their Google password. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid connecting sensitive accounts to this demonstration until its security has been reviewed.
